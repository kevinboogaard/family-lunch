🏆 Top 10 producten

SELECT product, SUM(aantal) GROUP BY product ORDER BY 2 DESC LIMIT 10

🗂️ Per categorie

SELECT categorie, SUM(aantal) GROUP BY categorie

⏱️ Wanneer wordt er besteld?

SELECT FLOOR(tijd / 10 min), COUNT(bon) GROUP BY 1

📈 Omzet per vrijdag

SELECT datum, SUM(totaal) GROUP BY datum ORDER BY datum

💞 Beste duo's

SELECT a.product, b.product, COUNT(*) FROM bon a JOIN bon b USING (bon) WHERE a < b
